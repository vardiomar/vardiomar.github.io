### 📄 Write-up 1 Title
<a id="writeup-1"></a>

**Course / Context:** CYSE/IT 200T - Cybersecurity, Technology & Society

**Date:** Fall 2026

🔗 [Read the full write-up](/YOUR_FILE.pdf)

**Summary:** This paper examines the CIA triad. It explains how authentication and authorization help protect information and systems. It uses the 2015 Anthem data breach to show how phishing and weak access controls can expose sensitive information.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Confidentiality protects information from unauthorized access.
- Integrity ensures data remains accurate and trustworthy.
- Availability ensures authorized users can access systems when needed.
- Authentication verifies who a user is, while authorization determines what they can access.
- MFA, access controls, employee training, and monitoring help reduce security risks.

### 📄 Write-up 2 Title
<a id="writeup-2"></a>

**Course / Context:** CYSE/IT 200T - Cybersecurity, Technology & Society

**Date:** Fall 2026

🔗 [Read the full write-up](/YOUR_FILE.pdf)

**Summary:** This paper explores how human behavior can create cybersecurity risks even when strong technical defenses are in place. It focuses on threats such as phishing, social engineering, weak passwords, and poor security practices, while emphasizing the importance of security awareness and organizational culture.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Human behavior is a significant part of the cybersecurity attack surface.
- Phishing and social engineering exploit human decision-making.
- Weak and reused passwords can increase the impact of credential compromise.
- Security policies should encourage employees to report mistakes and suspicious activity.
- MFA, least privilege, access controls, training, and monitoring can reduce human-related risks.

### 📄 Write-up 3 Title
<a id="writeup-3"></a>

**Course / Context:** CYSE/IT 200T - Cybersecurity, Technology & Society

**Date:** Fall 2026

🔗 [Read the full write-up](/YOUR_FILE.pdf)

**Summary:** This paper examines SCADA systems and their role in monitoring and coordinating critical infrastructure and industrial processes. It highlights how securing OT/ICS environments differs from traditional IT because cyber incidents can affect physical operations, public services, reliability, and safety.     SCADA_Systems_and_ICS

**Key Takeaways:**
- SCADA environments commonly include PLCs, RTUs, HMIs, and supervisory systems.
- OT security must account for safety, reliability, and operational requirements.
- Increased network connectivity can create additional attack paths.
- Patching industrial systems can be difficult because changes may affect operations.
- Network segmentation, strong access controls, monitoring, incident response, and recovery planning improve SCADA security.
